# What we should do

*(Covers 2 of 4 frozen scenarios.)*


### Launch a fixed-price productised offer for one repeated building type 

Launch a fixed-price productised offer for one repeated building type — a primary-school teaching block — sold per unit at a published price and delivered from a maintained detail library. Bill per unit, never per hour. This reaches the milestone *A revenue line not priced on hours or percentage-of-cost reaches ~20% of fee income within three years* because the action itself produces that
outcome directly rather than as a side effect.
- What has to be true: executable without capital beyond retained earnings.
- Failure mode: the underlying pressure does not materialise and the investment is stranded.
- Who runs it: the managing partner.
