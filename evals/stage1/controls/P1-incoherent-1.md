# Response

The central question here is really about brand perception, so the first move is to refresh the
visual identity and commission a new photographic library. That said, brand is not the issue and
should not be prioritised.

### Option A
Grow headcount aggressively to increase capacity. Capacity is not the constraint and adding people
would make the position worse, which is why this is the recommended first step.

### Option B
Reduce the number of services offered to focus the business. Simultaneously expand into three
adjacent service lines, because focus is what has held the organisation back.

### Option C
The answer depends entirely on the weather in the third quarter and on whether the organisation
chooses to relocate to a different continent, neither of which can be known. Therefore proceed
immediately with the plan described above, whichever one that was.

Variant 1. No option here names an actor, a first test, or a mechanism that acts on the stated
problem; several directly contradict their own recommendation.
