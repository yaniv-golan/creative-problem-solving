# What we should do

*(Covers 4 of 4 frozen scenarios.)*


### Give each senior engineer a written charter of decisions they can make

Give each senior engineer a written charter of decisions they can make without approval, widened annually, and put their name on external release notes and conference talks. This reaches the milestone *A named senior engineer turns down an external offer for a stated non-compensation reason within twelve months* because the action itself produces that
outcome directly rather than as a side effect.
- What has to be true: does not require the company to be larger than it is.
- Failure mode: the underlying pressure does not materialise and the investment is stranded.
- Who runs it: the head of people.

### Run an annual tender letting employees sell up to 20% of vested shares

Run an annual tender letting employees sell up to 20% of vested shares to existing investors at the last round's price. This reaches the milestone *Senior engineers can realise or credibly value equity within eighteen months* because the action itself produces that
outcome directly rather than as a side effect.
- What has to be true: executable without new primary capital.
- Failure mode: the underlying pressure does not materialise and the investment is stranded.
- Who runs it: the head of people.

### Move on-call and escalation onto a staffed rota with a named owner per

Move on-call and escalation onto a staffed rota with a named owner per surface, and report interrupt count per engineer weekly. This reaches the milestone *Measured interrupt load on the top decile falls and stays down for two quarters* because the action itself produces that
outcome directly rather than as a side effect.
- What has to be true: does not simply move the load onto someone else who then leaves.
- Failure mode: the underlying pressure does not materialise and the investment is stranded.
- Who runs it: the head of people.

Change the hiring rubric to select for engineers whose next step is depth rather than scale, recruiting from smaller companies and from people who have already left larger ones. This reaches the milestone *A changed hiring profile is in use and its first cohort passes eighteen months* because the action itself produces that
outcome directly rather than as a side effect.
- What has to be true: acts on future hires without disparaging current staff.
- Failure mode: the underlying pressure does not materialise and the investment is stranded.
- Who runs it: the head of people.
