# Intermediate artifacts — creative-problem-solving, deep mode

## Phase 0 — sharpened brief

**Function (form stripped):** A stranger has decided they want in. The operator needs enough
confidence about who they are to accept the risk of admitting them; the person needs to reach
what they came for. Today all confidence is purchased up front, before value, and 60% refuse.

**Adversarial attributes injected:**
- the person suspects the request is a phishing attempt and will not hand over a document or type a code
- the assurance bar is RISING, not falling
- the budget is FALLING; no re-platform, no new native app, small team
- most of the people lost were never going to be fraudulent

**Banned vocabulary:** verify, verification, onboarding, flow, funnel, drop-off, form, progress,
friction, step, signup, sign-up, user.

**Baseline (obvious answer):** defer the identity check until after first value, add social
sign-in and magic links, cut fields, send a resume-link reminder email, A/B test the copy.

**Retrieved neighbours (difference constraint, from 4 web searches):**
1. Social / SSO sign-in to inherit a checked identity
2. Progressive profiling
3. Deferred / lazy registration
4. Desktop-to-mobile QR handoff
5. Pre-fill from authoritative sources
6. Risk-based adaptive tiering
7. Magic links / OTP
8. Reusable digital identity credentials and wallets
9. Passkeys / biometric-bound credentials
10. Real-time inline field validation
11. Abandonment-recovery email or SMS with resume link
12. Better liveness capture UX
13. Trust badges, seals, explanatory copy
14. Vendor swap / vendor cascade
15. Live chat or human rescue at failure
16. Shortening the questions, adding a completion indicator

## Phase 2 — category negation

The 48 pooled candidates cluster into five, each with a named assumption:

- **A. Shift the burden of proof onto the operator** — assumes abandonment is fear of impersonation.
- **B. Harvest assurance from signals emitted anyway** — assumes passive signal reaches the bar.
- **C. Borrow assurance from an institution or person who already holds it** — assumes a third party will attest and carry liability.
- **D. Stop needing certainty; bound or price the downside** — assumes a risk-outcome standard, not a procedural must.
- **E. Invert the commercial relationship around checking itself** — assumes who pays for assurance is negotiable.

All five share a deeper assumption: that the 60% is caused by the assurance demand, and the
answer is to produce assurance differently or need less of it.

Negation round — five new clusters generated outside all of the above:
1. **Attack the composition of the arriving population, not the gate** (is the 60% one population, or four, one of which is bot traffic that should fail?)
2. **Change the psychology of walking away, not the cost of staying** (endow something losable before the ask)
3. **Timing as a resource — the request has a right and a wrong hour** (decline to ask when compliance is physically impossible)
4. **Route the loss elsewhere rather than prevent it** (convert abandoners into a lower-assurance product or partner)
5. **Redraw the product/entity boundary so the obligation attaches elsewhere**

Clusters 1 and 3 survived to the final set (1 as the opening diagnostic, 3 as idea "Ask at an
hour they can answer"). Clusters 2, 4 and 5 were cut in Phase 3.

## Phase 3 — pruning record

Merges: operator-proves-itself appeared in 4 generators; vouching in 4; passive accrual in 5;
money-rail in 3; exposure-bounding in 6. Merged to one each.

Cross-consistency kills: pre-built roster (no lawful basis); mark-recapture and Dorfman pooling
(no supervisor accepts a population bound; collapses into risk tiering); rugby advantage
(requires perfect reversibility); vendor-buys-traffic (not causable by a small team);
person-monetises-own-record (collapses into wallets, retrieved neighbour #8).

Grounding verification (deep-mode requirement): apiculture candy-plug queen introduction —
verified, standard practice, 3–5 days, mechanism is pheromone acclimation during a delay the
colony itself consumes. Matzinger danger model + Bretscher–Cohn two-signal rule — verified,
costimulation upregulated by tissue distress signals, antigen without signal 2 induces anergy.
Prior art verified for the money-rail idea (Trustly Pay N Play, penny-drop) and for reverse
authentication (concept + patents in banking).
