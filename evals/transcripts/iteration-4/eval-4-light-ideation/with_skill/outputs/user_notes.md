## Ambiguous or self-contradictory in the skill

1. **Diversity script threshold vs. fast mode.** Phase 3 step 1 says "skip it entirely in fast mode with fewer than ~12 short ideas." I had 21, so I ran it — and it returned precisely the uninformative near-100% the skill predicts. The threshold appears to be a proxy for pool size when the real predictor is *idea length and phrasing variety*, not count. In practice the script may be uninformative on any fast-mode pool.
2. **Convergence reporting vs. no-process-exposure.** Phase 3 step 1 says convergence "belongs in the answer rather than being silently deduped," but Phase 4 bans lens names, cluster analysis and process apparatus. Stating "five of six generators landed here" is exactly the process exposure Phase 4 forbids. Resolved by delivering the convergence as a substantive claim ("the two things that reliably move this are…"), but the skill doesn't say how to reconcile these and a less careful run would either leak process or drop the signal.
3. **Length budget vs. option count.** "Light question under ~600 words" collides with "4-8 options" plus the three-bullet template plus a cut list plus a closing read. Four options at the template's minimum viable depth already lands near 700. I cut to four options and trimmed prose; the response is ~690 words, moderately over the light-question budget. The skill's own instruction ("cut ideas rather than compressing all of them") was followed, but 4 is the template's floor — going to 3 would violate the other constraint. These two rules cannot both be satisfied on a light question.
4. **"Ordered by distance from the obvious answer, not by your confidence"** conflicts mildly with "present only live options" and with the closing line's demand for a ranking. The response orders by distance and then names its actual pick in the closing line, which seems to be the intent, but it means the numbered order is not the recommendation order — potentially confusing for a reader.
