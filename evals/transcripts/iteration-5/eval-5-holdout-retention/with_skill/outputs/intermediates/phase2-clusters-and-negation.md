# Phase 2 — category negation (working state)

Pool: 48 candidates from 6 blind generators (first principles, inversion, actor reversal,
constraint extremity, analogical transfer, biomimicry).

## Clusters the pool fell into

**A. Forced externalisation of the tacit model at commit-time.** Decision autopsy ledger,
executable-constraint encoding, chaos diary, stigmergic traces, hidden-move archive,
conclusive register, escrow payload. *Assumes:* the loss is knowledge, and the knowledge is
articulable by the holder.

**B. Induced failure / rehearsed absence.** Bus-factor drills, deliberate micro-necrosis,
blind reconstruction trial, rebuild-before-departure. *Assumes:* the organisation can afford
to break things on purpose and will read gaps as fundable defects rather than as blame.

**C. Structural coupling / forced pairing.** Two-signature decisions, hostage interfaces,
lichen-style bonded pairs, cross-feeding dependency, successor-as-customer. *Assumes:*
interdependence is cheap enough not to throttle throughput.

**D. Non-monetary standing that has no market price.** Guaranteed-nights claim, unilateral
fork right, successor-names-predecessor authorship, debt auction board, unfinished object.
*Assumes:* some internal resource is genuinely scarce and wanted, and a claim on it is
subjectively valuable.

**E. Pointing the person outward.** Rent them to rivals, customers hold the model,
third-party advisory. *Assumes:* an outside market pays for system-specific judgement.

**F. Detection instrumentation.** Interruption toll, quorum-threshold cascade, queen-cell
precursor reading, fragility on the balance sheet, reading the rival's approaches as a
price signal. *Assumes:* the departure or the dependency is measurable from behaviour
rather than conversation.

## What the whole pool missed

Every cluster answers sub-function (b) — stop the knowledge leaving — and almost none
answers (a) — why the person stops wanting to be here at month 18. That is my Phase 0
framing leaking into the generators: I stated the function in terms of "accumulated
judgement" and they followed the noun. The negation round is aimed at the gap.

## Negation round — 5 outside all of A-F

**N1. Shape the population, not the experience** *(new cluster: entry-side selection).* The
18-month curve is partly set at the moment of hiring; systematically bias intake toward
people whose honest next-best-alternative is this kind of place — people who have already
done the big-logo tour and are net sellers of prestige — rather than people whose
trajectory points at a larger name.

**N2. Treat unsolved problems as a stock with a depletion rate** *(new cluster: manage the
supply of frontier, not the person).* A capable person consumes the hard problems that
existed when they arrived at a measurable rate; if the organisation opens new fronts more
slowly than that, departure is arithmetic, not sentiment.

**N3. Compete on the thing a large organisation cannot approve** *(new cluster: beat them on
policy surface, not on the offer).* A 50x-budget rival wins anything with a price, but its
policy surface is rigid; the winnable ground is bespoke structural accommodation that one
person here can approve in an afternoon and a large company literally cannot.

**N4. Make the exit continuous rather than binary** *(new cluster: change the topology of
leaving).* Buy, in advance, a bounded post-departure claim on the person's time for their
old area, so the transition is a change in bandwidth rather than a change in state.

**N5. Change the payoff for early disclosure** *(new cluster: buy the information the
politeness constraint hides).* Make "I want something different within a year" trigger a
defined and generous response rather than a quiet downgrade in trust.

*Honest note:* N5 lands partially back inside the already-exists neighbour "stay interviews"
— different mechanism (payoff matrix rather than conversation ritual), same surface. Kept
with the caveat; not promoted to the answer as a headline option.
